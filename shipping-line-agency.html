Shipping Line Agency & Vessel Support

Coordinating Vessel & Cargo Operations

Big Sea Freight provides shipping line agency support and coordination for vessel and cargo operations. Our agency-related logistics support helps connect shipping lines, vessel operators, cargo owners, consignees, ports, terminals, and other parties involved in cargo movement.

Our Support Includes

From Arrival Through Onward Movement

  • Shipping line coordination
  • Vessel operation support
  • Cargo documentation
  • Port-related coordination
  • Cargo handling arrangements
  • Import and export shipment coordination
  • Communication with relevant stakeholders
  • Cargo movement follow-up
  • Operational assistance

Our Objective

Properly Coordinated From Arrival to Delivery

Our objective is to help ensure that vessel and cargo operations are properly coordinated from arrival through cargo handling and onward movement — connecting every party involved so cargo keeps moving without unnecessary delay.
