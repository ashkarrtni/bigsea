About Us

About Big Sea Freight

Big Sea Freight is a freight forwarding and cargo logistics company based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. We support businesses with international cargo transportation, shipping coordination, cargo handling, and logistics management, designed to help customers move goods safely and efficiently through reliable transportation networks.

With Dubai serving as a major international trade and logistics hub, Big Sea Freight is strategically positioned to coordinate shipments connecting businesses with regional and international markets. Our capabilities include ocean freight, air freight, road transportation, cargo consolidation, full-load services, customs coordination, warehousing, specialized cargo handling, and express delivery. We also provide shipping line agency and cargo operation support, helping coordinate vessel-related activities and cargo movements.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide dependable, efficient, and customer-focused freight and logistics solutions that help businesses move cargo with confidence. We aim to simplify international logistics through coordinated transportation, professional cargo handling, responsive service, and practical supply-chain solutions. We continuously work to improve our processes, strengthen our logistics network, and provide solutions that meet the changing needs of our customers.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted and recognized logistics partner for businesses seeking efficient cargo transportation and international freight solutions. We aim to build a stronger logistics network connecting businesses, markets, shipping lines, ports, airports, and transportation partners around the world — creating a smarter and more connected logistics experience through technology, operational efficiency, and long-term partnerships.
