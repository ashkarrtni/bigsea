About Us

Your Reliable Logistics Partner

Big Sea Freight is a Dubai-based freight forwarding and cargo logistics company. We simplify the movement of cargo by bringing transportation, documentation, cargo handling, customs coordination, and delivery support together under one logistics network.

Based in the United Arab Emirates, we support businesses with international freight forwarding and shipping solutions tailored to different cargo types, destinations, and delivery requirements — whether it's a single consignment or regular commercial cargo.
