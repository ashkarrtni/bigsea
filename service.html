Our Services

Complete Freight & Logistics Solutions

Big Sea Freight covers every stage of the shipping process — freight services, cargo consolidation, full-load transportation, logistics services, specialized cargo handling, and express delivery, all coordinated from Dubai, UAE.

01 — Freight Services

Air, Sea & Road Freight

Air Freight

Fast and dependable air cargo solutions for shipments where time matters. Big Sea Freight coordinates air cargo transportation for businesses requiring speed, reliability, and controlled shipment handling — suitable for urgent shipments, high-value goods, time-sensitive products, samples, and documents.

  • International Air Cargo
  • Airport-to-Airport & Door-to-Door Air Freight
  • Express Air Cargo
  • Documentation & Customs Clearance Assistance

Process:

  • Shipment Request
  • Routing Arrangement
  • Cargo Collection
  • Documentation
  • Air Transportation
  • Customs & Delivery

Sea Freight

Sea freight remains one of the most practical methods for transporting commercial cargo internationally. Big Sea Freight provides ocean freight forwarding and shipping coordination for businesses moving cargo between international ports — ideal for large commercial shipments, heavy cargo, bulk shipments, machinery, and containerized goods.

  • FCL & LCL Shipping
  • Port-to-Port & Door-to-Door Ocean Freight
  • Import & Export Shipments
  • Shipping Line Coordination

Process:

  • Cargo Booking
  • Documentation
  • Cargo Loading
  • Port Handling
  • Ocean Transit
  • Destination Clearance
  • Final Delivery

Road Freight

Road freight provides flexible connectivity for shipments moving within the UAE and across regional markets. Big Sea Freight coordinates road transportation for commercial cargo requiring flexible pickup and delivery arrangements — supporting general cargo, machinery, palletized cargo, and containerized shipments.

  • Full & Less-Than-Truckload Shipments
  • Local & Cross-Border Transportation
  • Container Transportation
  • Door-to-Door Trucking

Process:

  • Pickup
  • Loading
  • Transportation
  • Border/Customs Coordination
  • Destination
  • Final Delivery

02 — Cargo Consolidation

LCL & LTL Shipping

LCL – Less than Container Load

A practical solution for smaller shipments that do not require a full container. LCL allows smaller consignments to share container capacity with other compatible cargo, helping customers avoid paying for unused space.

  • Cargo consolidation & deconsolidation
  • Import & export LCL
  • Port-to-port & door-to-door options

Process:

  • Collection
  • Consolidation
  • Container Loading
  • Ocean Transport
  • Deconsolidation
  • Delivery

LTL – Less than Truck Load

Cost-efficient road transportation designed for shipments that don't require an entire truck. Cargo from multiple customers can be consolidated into a single movement where suitable — ideal for pallets and partial loads.

  • Local, regional & cross-border LTL
  • Pickup, delivery & consolidation
  • Shipment tracking support

Process:

  • Booking
  • Pickup
  • Consolidation
  • Transportation
  • Destination Handling
  • Delivery

03 — Full Load Services

FCL & FTL

FCL – Full Container Load

FCL provides customers with exclusive use of a shipping container for their cargo, suitable for businesses moving larger quantities of goods or cargo requiring dedicated container capacity.

  • 20-foot & 40-foot containers
  • Export & import FCL
  • Port-to-port & door-to-door delivery

Process:

  • Booking
  • Container Allocation
  • Loading
  • Port Handling
  • Ocean Transit
  • Customs Clearance
  • Final Delivery

FTL – Full Truck Load

FTL provides a dedicated vehicle for a customer's shipment, making it suitable for larger cargo volumes and direct transportation requirements, with fewer transfers and better control of delivery schedules.

  • Dedicated local, regional & cross-border trucking
  • Container haulage
  • Specialized vehicle arrangements

Process:

  • Truck Booking
  • Cargo Pickup
  • Loading & Securing
  • Direct Transportation
  • Customs/Border Processing
  • Delivery

Door-to-Door, Warehousing & Customs

Door-to-Door Delivery

Complete logistics coordination from the point of pickup to the final delivery location — one coordinated solution instead of managing multiple providers separately.

  • Supplier pickup & origin handling
  • Export documentation & freight forwarding
  • Air, sea, road & multimodal options

Process:

  • Int'l Transportation

  • Local Transportation
  • Final Delivery

Warehousing & Distribution

Logistics coordination for cargo storage, inventory handling, order preparation, and distribution to retailers, businesses, customers, or project locations.

  • Short & long-term storage

  • Dispatch & distribution support

Customs Clearance

Assistance with customs-related documentation and coordination to help cargo move through required clearance procedures efficiently, reducing delays and documentation errors.

  • Duty/tax & cargo release coordination

  • Declaration

  • Inspection (if required)

05 — Specialized Cargo Handling

Breakbulk, RoRo & Dangerous Goods

Breakbulk Cargo

Specialized transportation and handling for oversized, heavy, or non-containerized cargo such as heavy machinery, industrial equipment, steel products, and project cargo.

  • Cargo assessment & transport planning
  • Port coordination & heavy-lift handling
  • Documentation & customs coordination

Process:

  • Assessment
  • Transport Planning
  • Port Coordination
  • Loading
  • Ocean Transit
  • Delivery

RoRo – Roll-on/Roll-off

Efficient shipping solutions for vehicles, machinery, and other wheeled equipment that can be driven or rolled onto specialized vessels — cars, trucks, buses, trailers and heavy vehicles.

  • Vehicle shipping & vessel booking support
  • Port handling & documentation
  • Destination handling & delivery

Dangerous Goods – DGR Cargo

Specialized logistics support for regulated cargo — chemicals, flammable materials, batteries, gases and hazardous substances — in accordance with applicable transportation and safety requirements (IATA, IMDG, ADR, and local regulations).

  • Classification & documentation
  • Packaging & labeling coordination
  • Compliance support

Process:

  • Identification
  • Classification
  • Documentation
  • Packaging & Labeling
  • Transportation
  • Customs & Delivery
